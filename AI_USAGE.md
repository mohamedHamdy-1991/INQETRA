# Statement on AI assistance

INQETRA was developed by Mohamed Hamdy Ali with substantial assistance from
AI coding agents (GLM via the ZCode agent, and OpenAI Codex) working under his
direction. All design decisions, the research-model architecture, the dataset
curation policy and the non-negotiable provenance rules are the author's; the
AI agents implemented, tested and refactored code against written specifications
and acceptance checks.

Responsibility for the correctness, security and licence compliance of the
submitted software rests with the human author, who reviewed, ran and verified
the complete test suite (33 automated tests) and the documented quality-control
gates. No AI-generated content is presented as a research result: the AI features
inside INQETRA are deterministic-only by design (see "Key features" in the paper),
and the 751-dataset catalogue was curated from authoritative public sources with
human review, not generated.

Generated artwork (interface illustrations and the logo, `apps/web/public/img/`,
`assets/`) was produced with an image-generation model to the author's
specification and is distributed under the same MIT licence as the code.
