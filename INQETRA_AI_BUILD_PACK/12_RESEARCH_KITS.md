# Research Kits

Research Kits instantiate a **research graph**, not just a dataset list.

Minimum library: Urban Heat Island; Residential Overheating; Building Stock Retrofit; Housing Energy Vulnerability; Fuel Poverty; Net Zero Buildings; Climate Adaptation; Flood Risk & Housing; Walkability; 15-minute City; Accessibility & Public Transport; Housing Affordability; Property Market Analysis; Urban Deprivation; Health & Built Environment; Air Quality; Urban Green Infrastructure; Biodiversity; Heritage & Conservation; Brownfield Development; Planning Constraints; Renewable Energy; EV Infrastructure; Digital Divide; Urban Morphology; Land-use Change; Remote Sensing; Place-based Economic Analysis.

Each kit contains: purpose, suggested RQs, aim templates, methods, required/recommended/optional DatasetRequirements, typical variables, compatibility caveats, expected outputs, difficulty, geography support and version history.

Starter machine-readable kits are in `data/research_kits.json`.
