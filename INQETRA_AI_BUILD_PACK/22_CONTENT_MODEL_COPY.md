# UI Content & Copy Model

## Brand
**INQETRA**
Primary line: **Turn research questions into executable data plans.**
Secondary: Discover authoritative data, align it to your aims and methods, understand the gaps, and leave with a reproducible research plan.

## Core CTA labels
Start a Research Project; Explore Datasets; Add to Basket; Assign to Aim; Find Data for Gap; Compare; View Provenance; Generate Data Plan; Draft Abstract; Trace Evidence.

## Trust labels
Official; Academic; Community; Publisher verified; Harvested; Curator enriched; AI suggested; User supplied; Licence unverified; Access restricted; Stale link; Superseded.

## Warning language
Prefer explicit factual warnings: “No temporal overlap with project period” not “Bad dataset”; “Licence terms could not be verified” not “Unsafe”; “No shared identifier found; a spatial join may be possible” not “Incompatible”.
