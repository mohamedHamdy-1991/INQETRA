# INQETRA Neo-Brutalist Design System

## Design DNA
Warm editorial canvas + explicit black structural frames + zero-blur hard depth + flat semantic accent colours + dense research information with strict hierarchy.

## Tokens
Canvas #F6E8D2; Surface #FBFAF6; Ink #161616; Yellow #FCDD28; Orange #F36D30; Pink #FF4F85; Cyan #12C8B0; Violet #8167F5; Green #4BD14A; Muted #D8CCB9.

Default border 2px black; major shell/modal 3px; internal rule 1px. Shadows 3/5/8px offset black, blur 0. Radius 0–8px; pills only for tags/status. Spacing 8px rhythm. Motion 100–180ms transform/shadow.

## Semantic accent mapping
Yellow primary/attention; Orange action/harvester; Cyan data/GIS/measurement; Violet research relationships/selection/AI; Pink warning/missing/restriction; Green verified/pass/success.

## Typography
Heavy grotesk/sans display 700–900; neutral sans body 15–18px; monospace 11–14px for metadata/numerics. Hierarchy uses framing, spacing and accent—not weight alone.

## Brand mark
Use `assets/inqetra-logo-mark-v2.png` as the primary compact mark. Pair it with the INQETRA wordmark in the display typeface only when the interface has enough room. Keep generous clear space equal to half the mark width; do not add gradients, soft shadows, third-party marks or decorative illustration to it.

## Shell
Desktop: persistent 220–260px left rail, 2px separator; top utility/search row; dense 12-column work area. Tablet collapses selectively. Mobile one column with drawer/bottom navigation; 44px targets; reduce shadow offsets to prevent overflow.

## Prohibited
Gradients; glassmorphism; blurred/glowing shadows; random pastel palette; generic SaaS rounded cards; 20px+ card radii; colour-only state; unstyled chart/map controls.

## Data UI
Tables have 2px outer frame, strong header, 1px rules, sticky header, aligned tabular numerics. Charts: cream plot, black axes, discrete palette, flat bars with black outline, framed tooltip, accessible summary/table. Maps: warm land, cyan water, pale green parks, geometric outlined markers, framed controls/legend, discrete choropleths, preserved attribution.
